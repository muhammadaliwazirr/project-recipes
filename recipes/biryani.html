Description

Biryani is a well known dish mainly served in a country like pakistan
The spices that are used in it makes it a dish that everyone craves

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice (500g)
  • Meat/Chicken (500g to 750g)
  • Plain yogurt (1/2 cup)
  • Onions (2-3 medium)
  • Ghee or cooking oil (1/4 cup)

Steps

  1. Boil Rice
  2. Make Meat Gravy (Korma)
  3. Layer and Dum (Steam)
