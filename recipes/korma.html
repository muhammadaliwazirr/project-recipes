Korma

Description

Korma is a rich, mildly spiced South Asian curry
It features tender ingredients simmered with fragrant spices for a thick, velvety sauce.

Ingredients

  • 500g (1 lb) chicken or mutton
  • 1/2 cup oil or ghee
  • 2 medium onions (sliced and fried brown)
  • 1/2 cup whisked plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste

Steps

  1. Fry Onions
  2. Sizzle Spices
  3. Add Aromatics & Chicken
  4. Add Spices & Yogurt
  5. Simmer
  6. Finish
